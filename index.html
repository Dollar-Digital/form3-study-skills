Weekly check-in

Once a week, sit down together for a few minutes and talk through these three questions to plan the week ahead.

    Where to start

    One habit for each, and the school tool that helps.

    What’s due, and when?

    The habitCheck daily, plan weekly.
    Where it happens

    Teams, where most subjects post work

    Where did I save that?

    The habitKeep each subject in one place.
    Where it happens

    OneNote, OneDrive or a paper jotter, depending on the subject

    How do I know what to work on?

    The habitCheck in on your learning regularly.
    Where it happens

    Achieve, online support for N5 and Higher

    How-to guides

    New to Dollar, or just want a quick refresher? Short guides, one job each, ready whenever you need them.

    Can't find something?

    Each subject organises its work in its own way. If something isn't where you expect, the subject teacher is the best person to ask.

    More free tools that help

    Trusted, completely free extras for planning, studying and exam time.

    • Past papersReal exam papers and marking instructions for National 5 and Higher, from Qualifications Scotland.
    • BBC BitesizeShort guides, videos and quizzes. Choose Scotland and National 5 for the right course content.
    • Understanding StandardsReal marked answers with examiner comments from Qualifications Scotland, so you can see what a good answer looks like.
    • Exam timetable and study plan appsExam dates, plus the free MyExams and MyStudyPlan apps to build your own exam timetable and study plan.
    • PomofocusA study timer: 25 minutes of focus, then a 5-minute break. No account needed.
    • Immersive ReaderBuilt into Teams and other Microsoft apps. Reads text aloud and makes pages easier to read, which is great for listening learners.
